Dear Editors of *Research Synthesis Methods*:

Please consider our manuscript, “Contrast-Level Aggregate-Data Dose--Response
Meta-Analysis for Heterogeneous Exposure Intervals,” as a Research Article in
*Research Synthesis Methods*. Published studies may report only adjusted odds
ratios and confidence intervals for study-specific exposure ranges,
thresholds, or exact values. Conventional dose--response meta-analysis then
requires an assigned category dose, while methods that estimate interval means
usually require exposure-group frequencies or outcome counts. Those inputs are
unavailable in the setting considered here, and each reported contrast may
compare its own pair of supports.

We introduce an outcome-independent support-to-design operator for these
already-adjusted contrasts. It maps every comparison and reference support to
an expected dose--response basis under a defensible working exposure
distribution and retains the reported arbitrary pair and within-study
dependence. Positive-width intervals and exact values can be combined, with
exact values represented as zero-width supports. Conventional point-dose DRMA
and, under Uniform within-support exposure, midpoint DRMA are exact special
cases of the implemented linear model. The method separates first-stage
support assumptions from downstream GLS/profile-REML synthesis and includes
explicit procedures for first-stage uncertainty and diagnostics. No method
identified in our targeted search accepts the complete combination of
arbitrary adjusted support-pair contrasts, mixed points and intervals,
within-study dependence, and absence of group frequencies or outcome counts.

The evidence is deliberately broad and transparent. Under correct
specification, slope bias was negligible in 5,000-replicate mixed-support
simulations; severe exposure-distribution misspecification produced substantial
bias, establishing when the working model must be defended or the analysis
should not be used. The 16-study residential-radon illustration gives an OR of
1.081 per 100 Bq/m$^3$ (95% CI 1.032--1.133) using independent historical
country-specific Lognormal distributions, with CR2, correlation, influence,
and one-contrast-per-study sensitivities. The motivating AFO example is
reported as an eight-study working analysis of all mapped contrasts, followed
by separately labeled OR-only and strict explicit-support restriction
analyses. Published randomized-trial target-range meta-analyses show that the
same support-representation problem is not limited to environmental studies.

The paper fits RSM’s focus on new, generally useful research-synthesis methods,
simulation evaluation, software, and illustrative applications. The public
repository contains analysis-ready workbooks, core software, deterministic
tests, seeds, and machine-readable results. A permanent versioned archive DOI

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors approved
